Consents

Signed consents

Every signed consent with its version, text fingerprint and permissions, plus forms waiting for a signature.

This section's video is coming

Until then, the written guide is right here: the same text the app's Guide button shows.

When you need it

Before a service that needs consent, or when you must show what a customer signed.

Step by step

  1. 1

    Click Send a form; the signing link is sent in the chat, or the customer signs on site.

  2. 2

    Under Waiting, see unsigned forms and use Send the link again if needed.

  3. 3

    Under Signed, open a consent to see Health answers and Permissions, and get a Printable copy.

Common mistakes

  • The health-flags filter shows yes answers to health questions; always check them before the service.
