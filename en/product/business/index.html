Quotes and projects

Approval with an e-signature

The customer approves the quote or contract by typing their full name and ticking consent, on the page or in the chat, and a consent record is kept. A paper approval can be recorded in the panel.

  • Sign a quote or a contract
  • Signing by typing the full name in the chat can be switched off

Quote requests with photos and sizes

The customer's answers, photos, files and measurements from the chat arrive together as one quote request in the team's inbox, ready to price or to book a site visit.

  • A ticket opens for the team when a request arrives
  • Close or reopen a request
  • Your trade's journeys that collect a price request, estimate, measurements or a brief become a request even without a quote step
  • Team notes on each request

Line-by-line quote builder

Add lines from your catalogue or free lines, with units, quantities, a discount per line and on the whole quote (percent or amount), sections, and VAT-exempt lines.

  • Line total = quantity × unit price − line discount
  • Quote discount: percent or fixed amount
  • Duplicate a quote
  • Quote and request numbers with your own prefixes (default R, Q, P, CO)

Quote versions with changes shown

Every sent version is frozen; editing starts the next version, and the customer sees what changed since the previous one.

Validity and expiry reminder

Each quote has a validity date; the customer gets one reminder the day before it ends, then the quote expires on its own.

  • Default validity 14 days (1–365)
  • The reminder can be switched off

Quote page and printable copy

The customer opens the quote on the phone: lines, discount, VAT, total, payment plan, validity and terms. The same page prints on A4 or saves as PDF from the browser.

  • Persian, Arabic and English, right-to-left or left-to-right
  • Dates in your business calendar

Decline or ask for a change

The customer can decline with a reason or ask for a change; their next message is recorded as the change and the quote goes back to the team.

Deposit and milestone payments

After approval the project gets a payment plan: deposit and balance, full payment, or three steps. The deposit is asked for right away, with a reminder if unpaid.

  • Default deposit 50%
  • Payment reminder after 24 hours (0–168)
  • Mark a step paid or waived, or undo it, in the panel
  • Payment through the app's standard payment request or the Payments+ module

A payment plan for each quote

Set up to 12 payment steps per quote, each with its own title, percentage and due date. The last step takes what is left, so the plan always adds up to 100%.

  • Step kinds: deposit, milestone, balance
  • First delivery date 5 days after the start by default (0–365)
  • The deposit is asked for right after approval, or later by a journey step

Progress updates with photos

Each progress update with text, a percentage and photos goes to the customer and stays on their project page; outside the 24-hour window it goes with their next message.

  • Project page: progress, steps and payments, updates, change orders, review

Previews and revision rounds

Send a preview for approval; the customer approves or asks for changes. Each project has a set number of revision rounds and their use is counted.

  • Default 2 revision rounds per project
  • When the included rounds are used up, the next change request is flagged to the team as an extra (paid) round
  • Add or take back a round by hand in the panel

Change orders

Record extra work with its lines, price and effect on the deadline; the customer approves or declines it in the chat or on the project page.

Handover, balance and review

At the end a handover note with files is sent, the balance is requested and the customer rates 1 to 5 stars; you can also send your own review page link.

Project status and work steps

Each project has a clear status: waiting for deposit, active, on hold, handover, completed or cancelled. Each step is marked to do, in progress or done, and progress updates from it.

  • Start and due dates, notes
  • A project can also be opened from the panel without a quote
  • Cancel a project from the panel

VAT on quotes

Your country's VAT rate is applied automatically once your tax number is set, or you set the rate yourself; prices can be entered with or without VAT.

  • Country rate comes from the Payments+ module when it is on
  • Prices without VAT or including VAT

Sensitive documents (or all of them, your choice) are encrypted with AES-256; the key is kept outside the database and sealed with this computer's secure key store. ID numbers are encrypted and only the last 4 digits show.

  • A copy of the data folder alone opens nothing
  • Every file view is written to an access log
  • Staff-only access

Ready document checklists per service

A library of 43 ready checklists for visas, company formation, translation, legal and tax, insurance, medical, real estate and more. Each document has a hint, example, allowed types and size, and expiry rules, all editable.

  • 43 checklists built from 59 document types
  • Fitted to 110 trades
  • Required or optional items, file count limits

Secure upload link with the phone camera

The customer opens a private link, takes photos with the camera or uploads several files and sees progress; rejected documents can be uploaded again.

  • Link valid 30 days by default, renewed with each notice
  • The customer cannot download back what they uploaded
  • Uploads limited to 60 per 10 minutes per link
  • PDF or photo per item, up to 25 MB a file (10 MB by default) and up to 30 files per item
  • The phone camera opens directly for photos

Document review queue

Staff approve each document with its expiry date and ID number, or reject it with a reason the customer reads in their own language. It warns when a document is expired or valid for too short a time.

  • Example: a passport needs 180 days of validity

  • Expiry dates typed in your business calendar
  • Ready rejection reasons: unclear image, part cut off, expired, incomplete, name does not match, not an official copy, not the requested document, not valid long enough, other
