Today: what needs you now

One list of what needs you now

The top of the dashboard shows only what needs you: waiting customers, drafts to approve, payment receipts, today's tasks and hot leads left without a reply. Each row has the button to deal with it.

  • 5 kinds: waiting customers, drafts, receipts to review, tasks due today, hot leads gone quiet
  • the first 6 rows are shown, then «and N more»
  • up to 20 waiting customers, 20 drafts, 10 receipts, 10 tasks and 5 hot leads are gathered
  • refreshes itself; a handled row disappears right away
  • Buttons: Answer, Let the assistant answer, Send, Reject, Open, Review
  • Buttons right there: «I'll answer», «Let the assistant answer», «Send», «Review», «Open»
  • When nothing is waiting, it says every customer has been answered
  • Test customers are left out

How long each customer has waited

For each waiting customer you see the name, the app (Instagram, WhatsApp or Telegram), how long they have waited and their last message; the longest wait comes first.

  • wait shown as «just now», minutes or hours

Answer yourself or let the assistant reply

«I'll answer» opens the chat. «Let the assistant answer» hands the chat back to the assistant right away and it replies.

Approve drafts in one tap

AI drafts are settled right here with «Send», «Reject» or «Open». A draft the assistant is unsure of is marked as such.

  • a note says after how many minutes unreviewed drafts go out on their own (unsure ones never)

Hot leads gone quiet

A hot lead who wrote in the last week, has heard nothing from you for a day and has no open task is listed, highest score first.

  • up to 5 at a time

Receipts waiting for review

A payment receipt a customer sent that is not yet confirmed appears in today's list; «Review» takes you to the payments page.

Tasks due today and overdue

Follow-up tasks due today appear with the customer's name, and late ones are marked «overdue».

A clear «nothing needs you» sign

When every customer has an answer you see a clear message that nothing needs you right now, not an empty list.

Dashboard and key numbers

Numbers for your kind of business

Each specialised section you switch on adds its own number tiles to the dashboard, such as today's bookings for a salon, today's orders for a shop or occupancy for a stay.

  • Appointments: today's bookings (and upcoming), awaiting confirmation, no-show rate in 30 days, utilisation of today's working hours
  • Shop and orders: orders today (and awaiting payment), to ship, sales in 30 days, needs attention (returns, low stock, drafts to price)
  • Payments: collected in 30 days, receipts to review (and open requests), needing attention (overdue instalments, cash on delivery, refunds)
  • Quotes and projects: awaiting decision with its value, new requests (and drafts to price), active projects with balance due, and win rate
  • Stays and rentals: arrivals today (returns, in use), holds awaiting payment (reservations in 7 days), occupancy today
  • Events: events in the next 30 days (booked this week), dates on hold (guests without a reply), tickets in 30 days (check-ins today)
  • Courses: active students (awaiting payment, waitlisted), attendance in 30 days (sessions today, unmarked)
  • Documents and applications: open applications (waiting for the customer), documents to review, expiring in 30 days
  • Consents: valid consents (signed in 30 days), waiting for signature (expiring soon)
  • Field service: visits today (on the road or working), jobs waiting for dispatch
  • Shipping: open shipments (out for delivery, delivered today), needs attention (exceptions, late, claims), cash on delivery to settle
  • Listings: available (reserved, closed in 30 days), hot and new leads, viewing requests (viewings in 7 days)
  • Memberships: active members and renewals in 7 days
  • Donations: this month's total (gifts, monthly donors), awaiting payment
  • Business clients: receivables (open and overdue invoices), accounts to verify (new quote requests)

Sales funnel from first message to purchase

  • «AI replying (seconds) · queue» or «Ready · queue»
  • the same bar holds the auto-reply / manual approval switch and the AI model picker

Updates on its own

The dashboard and the top bar refresh themselves every few seconds; no need to reload.

  • every 2.5 seconds

Warning bar when something is stuck

When something stops messages from arriving, a coloured bar with the reason and the fix appears at the top: Instagram paused for now, a temporary public address, the message port taken by another program, or a problem with the master key.

  • Instagram paused: since when and the next check time; Telegram and WhatsApp keep working
  • temporary public address: red when Instagram or WhatsApp depend on it, with a «Get a fixed address» link
  • the port that receives messages is used by another program, and that program's name is shown
  • the master key is missing, unreadable or weakly protected, with a link to the security page

Counters next to menu items

The menu shows open counts: drafts waiting, payments to review, open tickets, unanswered comments, settings errors and overdue tasks.

  • Approval queue: answers waiting
  • Comments: comments still to handle

The panel updates itself

When a newer panel is ready, the page reloads itself; if you are in the middle of editing, only a notice with an «Update» button appears so nothing is lost.

Reports: AI, keywords and automations

Each automation's results, up to the sale

Each automation's card shows how often it fired today and in total, and a small funnel shows how many of those people got the reply, submitted a form and paid, with each step's share of the one before.

  • comment automations: matched today, DMs sent, button clicks and click rate; funnel: match, DM, button click, delivered, form, payment
  • DM automations: triggered today, total, people, repeated or skipped; funnel: triggered, delivered, form, paid
  • a form or payment counts when it comes within 30 days of the automation's message; only payments you confirmed count

Today's AI work and cost

Four numbers: replies today with the model's name, average time from message to reply, today's cost in dollars, and how many replies the checks corrected or stopped.

  • cost is an estimate at the providers' list prices
  • a model without a known price shows no estimate rather than a guess
  • the built-in local engine costs nothing

Log of every AI reply

For each reply: time, customer, their message, the reply sent, the tools the assistant used, the result, the model, reply time and cost.

  • the last 150 replies
  • results: sent, replaced, safe answer, error
  • filter: corrected / rejected only

Find the words you missed

Reviewing the «no match» messages shows the words customers use often that are not yet in your keywords or lexicon.

Notifications and alerts

Team alerts bell

The bell at the top shows your own and your team's alerts with an unread count; tapping one opens that customer's case.

  • «mark all read» button
  • the last 40 alerts in the list
  • alerts are kept for 90 days
  • Alert types: customer waiting, still waiting, draft reply ready, assigned to you, new task, task due, task overdue, form submitted, hot lead, follow-up, daily summary, flow
  • Mark all as read
  • Alerts are kept for 90 days
  • The same alerts also reach the phone app when it is linked

What you get alerted about

Every event that needs action creates an alert for the team member responsible for that customer, or for everyone when nobody is.

  • a customer is waiting for the team, and one reminder if nobody answers within 15 minutes
  • an AI draft is ready, or the assistant is unsure and waits for you
  • a form was submitted
  • a hot lead (with its score)
  • a customer or a task was assigned to you
  • a task is due now, or an hour late
  • a flow's «notify the team» step
  • 80% and 100% of the AI spending cap, once a day
  • the Instagram connection is about to expire and could not renew itself
  • a message could not be handled after repeated restarts
  • the smart setup or the nightly update has proposals to approve
  • a question customers keep asking that nobody has answered
  • the master key file is missing

Morning summary

Every day at your opening time a summary arrives: customers waiting, chats whose reply window closes today, hot leads, payments to review, unfinished payments and overdue tasks.

  • once a day, at your opening hour (09:00 if no hours are set), in your time zone
  • on by default; up to 5 names per line
  • Uses your business hours, otherwise 09:00 in your time zone
  • Names up to 5 customers per line
  • When there is nothing to report, nothing is sent

Alerts on Telegram with buttons

Every alert also reaches the Telegram of team members who linked their account, with buttons such as «I'll answer», «Let the assistant answer», «Send» and «Reject». When one person taps, the others see who took it.

  • replying to an alert in Telegram sends that text to the customer as the team's answer
  • The link code is valid for 10 minutes; sending the text «/staff CODE» also works
  • Unlink any time
  • An on/off switch for Telegram alerts in the follow-up settings
  • Needs the business's Telegram bot connected
  • A linked team member's chat is never treated as a customer
  • Link it from the Tasks page, the Settings page or the last step of the setup wizard
  • «Open in the panel» opens the customer page when the panel has a secure public address (https); otherwise it replies with the case number

Sound and Windows alert for a waiting customer

When a new customer starts waiting for the team, the panel plays a short sound, the tab title shows the waiting count, and if the app window is not in front Windows shows a notice and the window flashes.

  • the sound can be switched off
  • The sound can be switched off in Settings
  • Counts only chats handed to the team

Waiting-customer alerts on your own WhatsApp

When switched on, only «a customer is waiting» and its reminder go to the owner's personal WhatsApp. Off by default: it needs a Meta-approved message template and Meta bills each message.

  • the last send result is shown on the settings card
  • Template in Persian, Arabic and English; the panel language first, then another approved one
  • Off by default; switches on only after Meta approves the template
  • The last send result, success or error, stays visible on the card
  • Only waiting-customer alerts go to WhatsApp; Meta charges for each template message
  • Your number cannot be the bot's own number

Search everywhere

Search the whole panel

Ctrl+K or the search button opens one box that searches customers, chats, flows, forms, settings and the rest at once, with results grouped by kind.

  • keyboard: arrows to move, Enter to open, Esc to close
  • matches are highlighted
  • Finds: customers, conversations, flows, forms, form fields, topics the assistant recognises, automations, tags, stages, settings, library files, tickets, payments, items of enabled modules, panel pages
  • Actions: new flow, new form, new field, new DM / comment / story automation, upload a file, light / dark mode, panel language
  • Remembers the last 8 searches in this browser
  • Keyboard: arrows, Enter, Esc

Everything is searchable

Find a customer by name, current or former username, phone, email, city, tag or stage, and a chat by its message text.

  • customers and cases
  • chats (message text)
  • flows
  • forms and form fields
  • message intents
  • automations
  • tags and pipeline stages
  • settings items
  • media library files
  • tickets (#number, subject, summary)
  • payments (#number, payment gateway reference, package, amount)
  • items of every enabled specialised section
  • panel pages

Finds typos, Finglish and Arabizi

Search ignores Persian and Arabic letter variants, treats Persian, Arabic and Western digits alike, and still finds typos, Finglish, Arabizi and words typed on the wrong keyboard layout.

  • examples: «gheymat» finds «قیمت», «a7jez» finds «احجز», «rdlj» typed on an English keyboard finds «قیمت»

Quick actions from the same box

Start work straight from the search box: new flow, new form, new field, new DM, comment or story automation, upload a file, switch day or night theme, or change the panel language.

Recent searches

Your latest searches are kept in this browser so you do not retype them.

  • up to 8

Every drop-down list is searchable

Every pick list in the panel has a search box; type a few letters. The same forgiving search for typos and Finglish works here, and recent picks sit on top of long lists.

  • keyboard: arrows, Home, End, Page Up/Down, Enter, Esc
  • in lists with several choices, each pick shows as a small label
  • tag boxes suggest your existing tags
  • a full-width sheet on phones

Getting started, country and calendar

252 countries, none built in

The app is ready for any country; the list shows names in Persian, Arabic or English, sorted in that language's own alphabet, with no country pinned on top.

  • 252 countries and territories
  • 163 currencies, named in the panel language
  • every time zone, named in the panel language

Country detected at install

The app guesses your country from this computer's time zone and sets that country's currency, calendar and working week. You can change any of them later.

  • if nothing is detected: US dollar, world time (UTC) and the Gregorian calendar
  • a new install starts in English; the panel language is the first setup step

Each country's week start and weekend

Weekdays in the panel start on your country's first day of the week, and the example opening hours close its weekend and keep the day before open until 14:00.

  • example hours: working days 09:00 to 21:00

Phone numbers from any country understood

Whether a number is written with Persian or Arabic digits, with 00 or +, with a leading zero or the country's own international prefix, it is read correctly and stored in the standard international form.

  • countries sharing a calling code, such as +1, +7 and +44, are told apart by the number itself
  • mobile numbers are recognised
