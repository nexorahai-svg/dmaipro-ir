Appointment booking

Appointments booked in the chat

The customer taps «Book», picks a service and chooses one of the open times shown as buttons; the booking is saved without your team. Works on Instagram, WhatsApp and Telegram.

  • 3 to 9 open times offered per message (default 6)
  • Words like «نوبت», «حجز» or «book» open it
  • Telegram commands /book and /mybookings
  • The «Book» menu button can be switched off
  • Offered times are spread across the day, not only the first morning hours

Customer page to manage a booking

Every booking has a private link: the details, add to the phone calendar, change the time and cancel, all within your policy and without a login.

  • The page explains late-cancellation consequences before the customer cancels
  • Changes on the page are confirmed in the chat

Understands typed days and times

The customer can type «10:30», «tomorrow» or «Saturday evening»; the open times closest to the wish are offered first.

  • Persian, Arabic and English words and digits
  • Parts of the day: morning, noon, afternoon, evening
  • Same day first, then the next open days

Another day and a calendar page

«Another day» shows the days that still have open times and sends a link to a page with a month calendar where the customer picks the day and time.

  • Mobile-first page in Persian, Arabic and English
  • No login: a private link per offer

«My bookings» in the chat

The customer sees their upcoming bookings in the chat and moves or cancels one with a button.

  • Shown in the menu only to people who have a booking coming

No double bookings

Every time is checked again before it is saved; if two people tap the same time at once only one gets it and the other is offered another time.

Bookings made by the team

Staff see each day's open times in the panel and book for a customer; after a confirmation they can also book outside working hours.

  • Double-click an empty spot in the calendar for a new booking
  • Optional message to the customer

Day and week calendar by staff

Each staff member's or room's bookings show in a day and week view; dragging a booking moves it and the customer is told.

  • Holds, unpaid deposits and closed hours are marked
  • Holidays shown on the day header

Bookings list with search

Every booking with filters for status, staff and date and search by reference, name or phone; confirm, cancel, no-show, late and notes from the same place.

  • Statuses: held, awaiting deposit, confirmed, done, no-show, cancelled, rescheduled
  • Each booking keeps its history of moves
  • Copy the customer's booking link
  • Where each booking came from: chat, flow, panel or booking page
  • Show or hide test conversations

Everything on the customer record

Each customer's record has a tab for reservations, classes, events and tickets, membership and training program, and the panel's search finds all of them by reference, name or phone.

  • Search covers reservations, unit types, classes, enrollments, events, guests, tickets, plans, subscriptions, programs and exercises
  • Bookings, enrollments and subscriptions move the customer to the matching stage of your sales pipeline

Services, staff and opening hours

Services with duration, price and deposit

Each service has a duration, price, deposit, start interval, minimum notice, booking window and the staff who do it.

  • Duration from 5 minutes to 14 days
  • Deposit: none, fixed amount or % of the price
  • Booking window up to 730 days
  • Names in Persian, Arabic and English

Preparation and clean-up time

Time before and after each service is blocked without the customer seeing it, so bookings never run into each other.

  • Up to 600 minutes before and after

Staff, rooms, equipment and vehicles

Each staff member or room has its own calendar with weekly hours, breaks and a colour, or follows the business hours.

  • 4 kinds: staff, room, equipment, vehicle
  • Up to 6 time ranges a day
  • Up to 10 breaks
  • A resource with bookings is archived, not deleted

Home visits with travel time

For a home visit, travel time before and after is blocked in the staff member's calendar.

  • Travel time up to 600 minutes

Group sessions with seats

With a capacity above one, several people book the same time and it stays open until the seats are full.

  • Up to 500 seats per time

Days off and special hours

Set a closed day or special hours for the whole business or one staff member; special hours even open a public holiday.

  • Date ranges
  • Up to 6 time ranges on a special day

Public holidays closed automatically

No times are offered on your country's public holidays; switch any of them off or add your own closed days.

  • Countries: Iran, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman, Iraq, Turkey
  • Iran: the 28 official days, Solar Hijri and lunar
  • Eid al-Fitr, Arafat, Eid al-Adha, Islamic New Year, Prophet's Birthday, national days

Lunar holiday dates you confirm

Lunar holidays are computed with the Umm al-Qura calendar and marked approximate; each year you confirm or move the date to match your country's moon sighting.

  • A date can move up to 60 days from the computed one
  • Back to the computed date in one click

Your time zone and local calendar

All times follow your business time zone, including daylight-saving changes; Persian readers see Solar Hijri dates, Arabic readers Hijri (when your business uses it) or Gregorian, with their own digits.

  • Week starts on Saturday in Iran, Sunday in the Gulf, else Monday
  • Weekend days by country

Services recognised from the customer's words

Other names customers use are added to a service so a form answer or a message lands on that service automatically.

  • Up to 40 other names per service

Fair share between staff

When any staff member can do the service, the booking goes to the least busy one that day; showing the staff name to customers is your choice.

Full-day and multi-day services

A service of a day or more is booked by whole days and only takes consecutive working days.

Services from your trade pack

One click creates the services from your trade pack's options; the slot length and no-show rules are read from the pack and stay editable.

  • Without any services, a default length and your business hours are used

Minimum notice and booking window

Set how soon at the earliest and how many days ahead customers can book, per service or for all.

  • Defaults: 60 minutes notice, 60 days ahead
  • Start every N minutes, or automatic

Deposits, policies and reminders

Deposit before the booking is confirmed

When a service has a deposit, the time is held until payment and the payment request goes out in the chat; once the payment is confirmed the booking is confirmed.

  • While a receipt is being reviewed the time stays held
  • A rejected receipt gives the customer more time to pay
  • Until a payment method is set up, bookings are confirmed without a deposit

Provisional holds that expire

The chosen time is held for the customer for a while; if the payment or confirmation does not come, the time is freed and the customer is told.

  • Default hold 60 minutes; 120 minutes for a deposit or receipt
  • From 5 minutes to 7 days

Cancellation policy

Cancelling is free until a number of hours before; after that the deposit is forfeited, a fixed fee or a % of the price applies, or nothing.

  • Default: free until 24 hours before
  • A refund due creates a task for the team
  • A late cancellation is flagged to the team

Rescheduling policy

The customer can move their own booking until a number of hours before and a set number of times; the deposit and price move to the new time.

  • Default: 1 free reschedule up to 24 hours before
  • Up to 99 free reschedules

Late arrivals

Minutes late are recorded; past the grace time your rule keeps the booking, shortens the service or cancels it as a no-show.

  • Default grace time 15 minutes

No-shows

A missed booking is marked as a no-show; the deposit is forfeited, a fee is recorded or nothing, and the customer's record gets a «No-show» tag.

Reminders before the booking

Up to three reminders go out in the chat before the booking, with «I'll be there», «Reschedule» and «Cancel» buttons.

  • Default: 24 hours and 2 hours before
  • Your trade pack's reminder wording is used when it has one

Waitlist

When nothing suits, the customer joins the waitlist; when a booking is cancelled or moved, the freed time is offered to the first person waiting with a «Book this time» button.

  • Matched by service, day and staff
  • Can be switched off

After-visit messages and rebook nudges

Your trade pack's follow-up messages are tied to bookings: before the start, after the end and some time after the last visit; they drop out if the booking is cancelled or the customer already booked again.

  • Fields in the message: date, time, service, staff, reference, price, booking link
  • A no-show never counts as a visit

Automatic or team confirmation

Bookings without a deposit can be confirmed automatically or wait for the team; a task is created for the team to confirm.

Reminders that respect the 24-hour rule

Instagram and WhatsApp only allow messages within 24 hours of the customer's last message; outside it WhatsApp uses your approved template with service, date and time, and elsewhere the reminder is skipped and noted.

  • WhatsApp template with 3 blanks: service, date, time

Team tasks and record updates

Every booking, confirmation, move, cancellation and reminder is logged on the customer's record and their stage is updated; tasks are created for confirmations, refunds and late cancellations.

  • A reschedule the policy refuses becomes a task for the team

«Talk to us» and «Book again» buttons

When no time is free or the policy does not allow a change, the customer gets a button to reach your team; after a cancellation or an expired hold a «Book again» button is sent.
