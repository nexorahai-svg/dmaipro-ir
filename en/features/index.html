The AI that answers customers

92

  • Hello, thanks and “ok” answered at onceA greeting, a thank-you or a short “ok” gets an instant reply without AI: a newcomer's hello gets your welcome and menu, a thank-you gets two next steps, and an “ok” right after a form link points back to the form.
  • Ready answers for your tradeFor 152 trades, the questions customers of that trade usually ask come with ready answers in three languages, switched on with your trade pack.
  • A general answer until you write your ownFor trade questions only you can answer, a general answer that is true for any business (“it depends on …; tell us this and our team confirms”) goes out until you write your own. Your answer always wins.
  • Your own FAQ, answered instantlyEvery FAQ question and answer you write is available to the AI at once and used word for word. Mark it instant and it is sent without AI, in your own words, for other ways of asking it too.
  • Two questions in one messageA message like “hours and prices?” gets both answers separately. If not every part has a ready answer, the whole message goes to the AI or your team; no message is half answered.
  • No wrong ready answerMatching is strict on purpose: when two topics are close, or your information is missing, no ready answer goes and the message continues as usual.
  • Understands meaning, not just wordsWhen the words do not match, the meaning of the message is compared with the ready questions: “till when are you open today” is “opening hours”. It handles slang, typos, Finglish and Gulf Arabic, on this computer, without internet.
  • Example hours are never told to customersThe app's example opening hours are not used in answers or given to the AI until you confirm or change them, with a “these are our real hours” button.
  • What is still missing, listedEach ready answer shows the languages it is ready in and what information the others need, with a direct link to where you fill it in.
  • Try a questionType a question and see what would be sent at once, in how many milliseconds, and whether it was found by meaning. Nothing is sent to anyone.
  • 10 editable default messagesSystem direct messages are ready; each can be switched on or off, has its own text per language, and a repeat interval per person.
  • Business hours with several ranges a daySet one or more time ranges for each weekday in your time zone. After hours a special message tells the next opening time (“tomorrow at 9”).
  • “One moment” only when neededWith the local engine the “one moment” message goes at once; with a cloud AI only when the reply takes over 8 seconds. Not repeated if a message went in the last 5 minutes.
  • Typos, Finglish and the wrong keyboardKeywords are found even with a typo, a suffix, Finglish (“gheymat”) or typed on an English keyboard (“rdlj” for “قیمت”), and each match says how it was found.

  • Look-alike words are not confusedTypos are accepted only in long words, real look-alike words are never treated as one, and an English message is never read as Finglish.
  • 36 ready message intentsThe app recognises what a message is about: price, booking, order status, shipping, payment, complaint, a request for a voice note and more, in Persian, Finglish, Arabic including Gulf dialect, Arabizi and English.

  • Your trade's own wordsThe installed trade pack and the enabled modules add their own words and intents to message recognition.
  • Synonym lexiconSynonym groups, colloquial forms and Finglish let one keyword catch all its forms. Groups are editable and also used to search your business information.
  • The AI answers only from your informationServices and prices, your business text, FAQ, contacts, payment methods, opening hours, address and shop products become small pieces, rebuilt on every save.

  • Knows what day it isToday's day and date in your business's calendar and time zone (the Persian solar calendar in Persian) go with every message, so “are you open tomorrow?” is answered right.
